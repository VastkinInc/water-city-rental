Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before partnering with us.

How quickly can I start earning?

Once you list your boat, customers can book within 24 hours. Most owners receive their first booking within the first week of listing.

What are the fees?

Zero joining fees during our launch phase. We take a small service fee per booking — see the Earnings section for details.

Do I need a captain?

All Water City Rental charters include a USCG-licensed captain. You can recommend one for your boat or let customers choose from our verified captain pool.

What about insurance?

You're responsible for your own boat insurance. We recommend marine liability coverage. Contact our team for partner insurance referrals.

When do I get paid?

Payouts are processed within 24 hours after each completed trip. Funds typically arrive in your bank account within 2-3 business days.
