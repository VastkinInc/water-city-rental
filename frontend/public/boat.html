About this Yacht

Experience the pinnacle of Mediterranean sailing aboard the Azure Serenity. This meticulously maintained Beneteau Oceanis 42.1 combines effortless performance with the comfort of a high-end coastal villa.

Whether you're seeking a romantic sunset cruise or an adventurous week-long exploration of the Balearic Islands, the Azure Serenity offers unparalleled stability and style. The interior features triple-cabin luxury with artisanal teak woodwork and ambient lighting designed to mirror the soft glow of the horizon.
